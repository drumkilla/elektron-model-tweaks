03 Get it onto the device

Recommended: send the file with Elektron Transfer, the fastest way. Any SysEx tool works too.

Or send it from this page over USB MIDI (Chrome / Edge)
  1. Connect the device over USB and open its OS upgrade screen (CONFIG > UPGRADE) so it waits for the firmware.
  2. Pick its MIDI port and press Send. Keep this tab in front until it is done.
Pace of a MIDI cable times this. Raise it if the transfer stalls.

Before you flash

  • Modifying the firmware voids your warranty and could brick your device. You do this at your own risk.
  • Make sure you can reach STARTUP MENU: power off, hold FUNC, power on, then OS UPGRADE. That is the way back to stock firmware.
  • Back up your projects first.
  • Every check of the command-line tool runs here too: the file is parsed in full, the MAIN OS must match the known original, every patched region is confirmed first, and the built file is re-verified (packet checksums, container checksums, HMAC) before you can download it.
