# Trig preview

With the sequencer **stopped or paused**, hold a step's trig key and press **PAGE**: the
step sounds with its own note, length, p-locks and sound lock. The page does not turn.

- It sounds regardless of the trig condition and probability, and through a muted
  track.
- Paused (PLAY pressed during playback) counts as stopped; thanks to 18nelli for this
  fix.
