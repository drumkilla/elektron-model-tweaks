# Latching mute

- Hold **TRACK** and tap **FUNC**: the mute mode latches and the FUNC key lights up.
- A short tap on **FUNC** alone leaves the mode.
- Combinations with FUNC do not leave it, menus such as FUNC + PAGE included, nor does
  holding FUNC to turn the encoders fast.
- TRACK, RETRIG and PATTERN bank select keep working while it is latched.

Holding FUNC to mute tracks behaves exactly as on stock firmware.
